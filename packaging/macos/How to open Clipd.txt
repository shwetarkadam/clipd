How to open Clipd
=================

Clipd is free and open source, so it isn't notarized by Apple (that needs a
paid developer account). macOS asks you to confirm it once. After that it
opens like any other app.

1. Double-click Clipd.
   macOS says "Apple could not verify Clipd is free of malware". Click Done.

2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security. Scroll down to Security and
   click "Open Anyway" next to "Clipd was blocked". Enter your password.

3. Clipd opens and offers to move itself into Applications. Click
   "Move to Applications". Then grant Accessibility and Input Monitoring
   when it asks: multi-slot copy and paste needs them.

On macOS 14 or older, step 2 is quicker: right-click Clipd, choose Open,
then click Open.


Prefer no warnings at all? Paste this into Terminal instead. It installs the
same build and macOS opens it without asking:

    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shwetarkadam/clipd/main/install.sh | bash
